Education
======
* **Ph.D. in Experimental Particle Physics**, Institute of High Energy Physics, Chinese Academy of Sciences, 2018–2023
  * Dissertation: *Study of hyperon–antihyperon pair production and their decay mechanisms at BESIII*
  * Advisor: Prof. Hai-Bo Li
* **B.S. in Physics**, Soochow University, 2014–2018
  * Advisor: Prof. Xinping Xu

Appointments
======
* **Postdoctoral Researcher**, Department of Physics, Indiana University Bloomington, March 2026–present
* **Postdoctoral Researcher**, Institute of High Energy Physics, Chinese Academy of Sciences, June 2023–February 2026

Research interests
======
* Hyperon and antihyperon physics
* Antihyperon–nucleon interactions
* CP-symmetry tests with entangled hyperon–antihyperon pairs
* Hyperon weak decays, isospin symmetry, and the \(\Delta I = 1/2\) rule
* Strange hadrons and hadron spectroscopy
* Charmonium decays and nonperturbative QCD
